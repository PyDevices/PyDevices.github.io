# PyDevices.github.io docs

The repository behind <https://pydevices.github.io/>. Most pages here are
generated from a database in the `.github` repository, so these pages are about
where each thing comes from and how to change it.

## Get started

- [Preview the site](get-started/install.md): clone it and serve it on your computer.

## Guides

- [Regenerate the pages](guides/regenerate-the-pages.md): after a change to the database, the shared assets or the PyScript template.
- [Rebuild the WebAssembly runtime](guides/rebuild-the-webassembly-runtime.md): refresh the MicroPython that runs the live demos.

## Reference

- [What is in the repository](reference/contents.md): every path, who writes it, and what the generator may overwrite.

## How it works

- [How the site is made and published](how-it-works/index.md): the database, the generator, the hand-written parts and the Pages deployment.
