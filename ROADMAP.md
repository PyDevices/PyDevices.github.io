# Roadmap

The portal at [pydevices.github.io](https://pydevices.github.io/) follows the
packages it describes, so most of its changes arrive with theirs.

## Next

- The site moves to the PyDevices domain, with the documentation, the package
  index, the examples and workbench each at their own address beside it.
- **Install your own build from the firmware page.** "Install from a file"
  takes a `.bin` you built and writes it to your board from Chrome or Edge,
  the way the page installs the released images.

Something wrong or missing on the site? Open an
[issue](https://github.com/PyDevices/PyDevices.github.io/issues).
