# Roadmap

The portal at [pydevices.github.io](https://pydevices.github.io/) follows the
packages it describes, so most of its changes arrive with theirs.

## Next

- The site moves to the PyDevices domain, with the documentation, the package
  index, the examples and workbench each at their own address beside it.
