audiodsp — CircuitPython-compatible audio for MicroPython and CPython.

audiodsp CircuitPython's audio system (audiocore, synthio, audiomixer, effects, audiomp3) ported to MicroPython and CPython, extending CircuitPython along the way: dynamics, routing, convolution, and offline rendering. On PyPI as pydevices-audiodsp; the instrument and effect libraries built on it ship from audiocomponents.
